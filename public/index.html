Choose one of the four dates, then tap Cast my vote. Your vote is remembered in this browser, so there’s no account to create.

Changed plans? Select another date and update your vote. It replaces your previous choice, keeping your total at one vote.

Each percentage is a date’s votes divided by all votes. The highest percentage leads. If dates are tied, they’re shown as joint leaders so the family can decide together. Rounded percentages may total slightly above or below 100%.
